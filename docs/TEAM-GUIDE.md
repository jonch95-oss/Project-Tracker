# Project Command: team guide

The app is at **https://ariel-dev-projects.vercel.app**. The same guide is in the app at **/guide**.

## 1. Get in (2 minutes)
- **Sign in** with your email or your sign-in name (for example `ariel`) and the password you were given. The first time, the app asks you to choose your own password (10+ characters).
- **Put it on your iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Step-by-step pictures: **/install**.
- **Face ID:** Settings → *Face ID & passkeys* → Add. After that, Face ID signs you in.
- **Notifications:** allow them when asked (iPhone: only after adding to the Home Screen). Quiet hours and what you want to hear about are in Settings → *Notifications*.

## 2. Your day: My Tasks
Everything you have to do, in order: **Overdue · Today · This week · Later · Waiting on others · Awaiting my approval**.
- **Tick** a task to finish it. Tap it to comment, @mention someone, attach a file or photo, or mark it *Waiting on* a third party.
- A task that **needs approval** goes to the approver when you tick it; it's done once they approve.
- Blocked tasks show what they're waiting for.

## 3. Projects
**Portfolio** shows every project you're on: its phase, progress, what's next and what's stuck, with a **Needs you** list at the top. Open a project for its tabs: **Overview · Checklist · Team · Dates & Expiries · Files · Construction · Units · Public Records · Activity**, and **Financials** only if you've been given access.
- **Construction:** daily log, schedule, RFIs, submittals, drawings, meetings and punch lists.
- **Photos:** *Take photo* opens the camera; photos are dated, and *Add location* is optional.

## 4. Find anything
Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows, or **/**) or use the **Search** tab on the iPhone. Type part of a project name, an address, a **BBL**, a task, a file or a person.

## 5. On site with no signal
Screens you've opened stay readable. **Ticks and comments** made offline are kept on your phone and sent when you're back online; if someone changed that task in the meantime, the app asks you first. **Sign out** on a shared phone: it removes everything of yours.

## 6. Who sees what
- **Money** (budgets, invoices, the Financial folder) only if the owner turned on *Financials* for you on that project.
- **Outside collaborators** (architects, GCs, consultants) see only their own tasks and the folders shared with them.
- **Investors and lenders** see only their portal.
- Every change is recorded in the audit log.

## 7. Sharing
Task and alert screens have a **WhatsApp** button that shares a link (never dollar figures). Email is off for now: invitations are links the owner sends you.

## 8. For the owner
- **Weekly report:** every Monday at 7am (New York), one page per project; open it from the menu or download the PDF.
- **Team:** invite people, set roles and per-project access (Financials, checklist edits, approvals), deactivate. Sign-in names for people without email: GitHub → Actions → *Create account*.
- **Audit log** and **System** (usage against the free limits, backups, job runs).

**Something wrong?** Close the app and open it again, or sign out and back in. Still stuck: tell Jon, with a screenshot.
